OUR STORY

More Than a Photo.
A Memory.

Mylin & Co. was created with one purpose — to turn life's most meaningful celebrations into memories you can keep.

Inspired by family and a love for beautiful celebrations, Mylin & Co. brings an elevated, interactive mirror experience to weddings, graduations, corporate events, community celebrations, and life's unforgettable milestones.

From the smallest details to the final keepsake, we believe your event deserves an experience that feels personal, polished, and uniquely yours.

Based in Sheboygan, Wisconsin, we proudly serve celebrations throughout the surrounding area with an experience designed to bring people together, capture genuine moments, and leave guests with something worth remembering.
